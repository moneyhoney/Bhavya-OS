# Diagnostic Route Context

`app/learning/diagnostic/` owns the thin route boundary for the optional local starting diagnostic. The interactive implementation lives in the sibling `Diagnostic.tsx` component.

The diagnostic samples four concepts from the proposed Foundation path, gives a hint before a retry, and stores weak/strong concept signals on the learner's device. It is orientation, not a validated assessment, official curriculum approval, or a measure of learner potential.

Keep the route lightweight and honest. Changes to questions or explanations are original proposed educational content and should follow `curriculum/CONTEXT.md` and `skills/bhavya-source-integrity/SKILL.md`.
