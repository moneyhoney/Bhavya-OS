# Proposed AI Learning Resource Map

Status: research and architecture proposal. This is not approved Foundation curriculum.

## Use policy

Free access is not the same as open licensing. Bhavya should prefer linking to third-party resources, create original explanations and activities, and preserve attribution/licensing metadata. No external lesson text, video, illustration, exercise, branding, or proprietary asset is copied here.

| Resource | Subject / audience | Free or open status | License / reuse implication | Proposed Bhavya use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Scratch](https://scratch.mit.edu/help/about/) | Creative coding, stories, games; especially ages 8–16 | Free access | Platform/assets have their own terms; verify each asset and license before reuse | Link as a making tool; create original pattern and storytelling activities |
| [Raspberry Pi Learn](https://www.raspberrypi.org/learn/) | Computing, coding clubs, projects, AI; young learners and educators | Free resources | Resource-specific terms apply; do not assume all content is adaptable | Link to project pathways; use objectives as inspiration for original lessons |
| [Experience AI](https://experience-ai.org/) | AI literacy and ML concepts for school-age learners and educators | Free access | Educator materials may be CC BY-NC-ND 4.0; no adaptation without permission | Link and attribute; do not remix protected materials |
| [Teachable Machine](https://teachablemachine.withgoogle.com/) | Browser-based image, sound, and pose model experiments | Free web tool | Tool and model/runtime terms must be checked before embedding or redistribution | Link or build an original experiment around classification concepts |
| [CS50 Beyond](https://cs50.harvard.edu/beyond/license/) | Computing and AI foundations | Free access | CC BY-NC-SA 4.0: attribution, non-commercial use, ShareAlike for adaptations | Link; adapt only with license review and attribution |
| [Code.org curriculum](https://code.org/en-US/about/code-org-free-curriculum-commitment) | K–12 CS, AI, and data science | Free and openly licensed for stated non-commercial use | Creative Commons terms apply; commercial use requires permission | Link or adapt selected concepts after license and attribution review |
| [Khan Academy](https://support.khanacademy.org/hc/en-us/articles/202260114-Does-it-cost-money-to-use-Khan-Academy) | Broad foundational learning and practice | Free access | Content reuse is not implied by free access; link unless rights are separately confirmed | Link for prerequisite practice and low-connectivity inspiration |
| [MIT OpenCourseWare](https://ocw.mit.edu/) | University-level computing and science | Free access | Course/material-specific licenses apply; verify before reuse | Link for advanced references; create age-appropriate original explanations |

## Review fields for future resources

Before incorporation, record: source URL, publisher, audience, subject, access cost, exact license, attribution, adaptation rights, commercial restrictions, ShareAlike/NoDerivatives terms, whether only linking is allowed, and the proposed original Bhavya artifact.

## Safeguarding note

Community-inspired check-ins and show-your-work systems must be moderated and controlled for young learners. No public child-to-child messaging is part of this proposal.
