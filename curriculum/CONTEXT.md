# Curriculum Context

## Purpose
`curriculum/` contains proposed, reviewable learning architecture and external-resource provenance. It is not approved Foundation curriculum.

## Belongs here
Learning-path proposals, module objectives, activity concepts, source/license metadata, and review status.

## Does not belong here
Copied third-party lessons, unreviewed AI output, student records, credentials, or published institutional policy.

## Inputs and outputs
Inputs are the PRD, governance/brand references, learning research, and first-party external resource documentation. Outputs are human-reviewable proposals for future curriculum development.

## Constraints
Every module is proposed until Foundation review. External resources may be linked, adapted, or quoted only as the recorded license permits.

## Status
Architecture and resource map proposed; no official course is published.
