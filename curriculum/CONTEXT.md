# Curriculum Context

## Purpose

`curriculum/` is the authoring and review room for AI-education content, progression, exercises, experiments, projects, and source provenance.

## Content status boundary

- **Original educational content:** the project may author generic explanations, examples, activities, and simulations for concepts such as algorithms, data, models, evaluation, verification, and responsible AI.
- **Foundation-approved curriculum:** content becomes official only after the Foundation's required human review and approval. Nothing in this room should imply that approval has happened.

Every artifact must display or record its status: proposed, in review, approved, published, deprecated, or rejected.

## Current state

- **Implemented:** proposed six-module learning outline and `RESOURCE-MAP.md` with external source/license treatment.
- **Partial:** learner-facing route content exists under `app/learning/`, but the content lifecycle, educator review workflow, localisation, and assessment model are not complete.
- **Not started:** approved course catalogue, educator authoring tools, versioned publication workflow, learner accounts, and curriculum analytics.

## Lesson anatomy

A review-ready lesson should define learner, objective, prerequisite, explanation, concrete example, visual representation, interaction, feedback, practice, reflection, completion condition, and next step. Prefer Understand → Observe → Try → Experiment → Build → Reflect. Design phone-first and identify what can be done without a computer versus what may later belong in an AI Lab.

## Source and licensing workflow

Use first-party sources for research. Free access does not imply open reuse. For each external resource record audience, subject, access, exact license, attribution, adaptation rights, restrictions, and Bhavya treatment: link, reference, adapt under license, or create original work. Never copy lessons, exercises, media, branding, or datasets without clear permission.
