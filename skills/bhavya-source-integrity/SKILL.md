# Bhavya Source Integrity

## Purpose
Protect Foundation truth, provenance, licensing, and attribution across product and learning work.

## Trigger
Use when adding facts, images, external educational resources, copied code, policy language, or research-derived artifacts.

## Inputs
Candidate source, intended use, authoritative Foundation hierarchy, and current resource/provenance map.

## Workflow
1. Classify the source as authoritative Foundation material, external reference, or original work.
2. Verify the source and license from a first-party location.
3. Record subject, audience, license, attribution, permitted reuse, and Bhavya treatment.
4. Preserve required attribution and keep external content distinct from Foundation-owned content.
5. Escalate unsupported claims for human review.

## Outputs
Updated provenance/resource map or a clear decision to link, reference, adapt under license, or create original work.

## Validation
Search the diff for unsupported statistics, locations, partnerships, testimonials, policy claims, copied content, secrets, and untracked assets.

## Failure conditions
Reject unclear licensing, dummy reference-image data, fabricated Foundation claims, or copied material whose permitted adaptation is unknown.
